📲 Install kaise karein

  1. Download button dabao.Chrome puchhe "Download karna hai?" to Download dabao.
  2. Download hone ke baad file kholo.Notification par tap karo ya Files → Downloads me jao.

  4. Google Play Protect warning aaye to More details → Install anyway dabao.Ye warning har us app par aati hai jo Play Store se nahi aata.
  5. Open dabao. Ho gaya! 🎉
